## Projects

### [Carleton Course Map](https://carletoncoursemap.ca/map) · [source](https://github.com/zaidahmad16/CarletonCourseMap)

Degree planner used by 1,700+ students. Scrapes Carleton Central, models AND/OR prerequisite logic as a graph, and stores 3,218 courses, 304 programs and 8,882 prerequisite edges in Postgres. API queries run under 2 ms.

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Next.js` `React Flow` `dagre`

### [OntarioDriveTestMap](https://ontariodrivetestmap.fyi) · [source](https://github.com/zaidahmad16/OntarioDriveTestMap)

Free map of Ontario road test routes. Reconstructs 13 routes across 3 test centres from Reddit posts and dashcam video using OCR, an offline index of 19,482 OpenStreetMap junctions and HDBSCAN clustering. Every segment comes with a confidence label.

`Python` `PaddleOCR` `pyosmium` `HDBSCAN` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `Leaflet`

### [Salah](https://github.com/zaidahmad16/Salah) · [write-up](https://zaidahmad.dev/projects/salah)

Prayer times app for the Linux Mint desktop. Shows your mosque's own published timetable, and when it can't download that, reproduces it from a calculation method fitted to the timetable: 99.2% of times exact against my mosque, never more than a minute off. GTK4 window plus a Cinnamon panel applet with reminders.

`Python` `GTK4` `libadwaita` `GJS` `Cinnamon`

### [Nintendo DS Business Card](https://github.com/zaidahmad16/DS_Business_Card)

A business card that boots on a Nintendo DS. Play a short game and get my contact info at the end. Written in C.

`C` `NDS SDK` `ARM`

### [Ghost Hunting Simulation](https://github.com/zaidahmad16/COMP2401-Final-Project)

Multithreaded simulation in C. Each actor runs on its own POSIX thread over shared memory, no deadlocks after 1,000+ ticks.

`C` `POSIX Threads` `Semaphores`
